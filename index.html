What we saw

Page views, product views, add to carts and checkouts are all sent to Google Ads as conversion events. Two Google Analytics IDs load on the site; live traffic reaches only one.

What it costs you

If a browsing action counts as a main goal, automated bidding pays for visitors who look but do not buy, and reported results look better than what reaches your bank. A second, silent Analytics property invites two versions of the truth.

How we fix it

  • Purchases with real order value as the only main goal
  • Browsing actions kept for insight only
  • One Analytics source, checked against Shopify weekly
  • New vs returning customer split

What it costs you

Shoppers compare in one glance. A product with 4,600+ reviews looks pricier than it is and carries no sale badge, even while you run B3G1 and a free body cream offer. Fewer clicks, lower conversion rate.

How we fix it

  • Lead with the best value size in the feed
  • Merchant promotions so B3G1 and gift offers show on the listing
  • Titles built around size, benefit and shade
  • Same check across every hero product

What we saw

Recent social campaigns lead with "Clean without compromise" and ingredient science. Several Google text ads lead with "Organic Makeup" and "Toxin-Free", and one promotion reads "100% off B3G1".

What it costs you

Mixed messages weaken the new positioning your team is investing in. Broad "toxin-free" style wording also draws closer review from ad platforms and shoppers.

How we fix it

  • Ad copy and assets rebuilt on the current story, with your content and design team
  • Claims kept inside Google beauty and health policies
  • Promotion text that reads clearly
